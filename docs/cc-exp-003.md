# CC-EXP-003 v1 · prospective BTC/USDT range breakout

Frozen before evaluation. Hypothesis: a break above yesterday's observed range can persist for one day. Test spot long only, using WhiteBIT public BTC_USDT five-minute book samples. Shadow remains ABSTAIN throughout collection. No orders, parameter search, early promotion or extension.

## Clock and evidence

T0 = 2026-11-21 00:00 UTC inclusive. End = 2027-11-21 00:00 UTC exclusive (365 days). First 21 full UTC days are warm-up only. Ignore pre-T0 data even for warm-up. 2020 through the protocol freeze was previously studied or available for design. Evaluate once after close of the window. An initial live collector check before T0 is commissioning only.

Source: https://whitebit.com/api/v4/public/orderbook/BTC_USDT?limit=5 . Expect one observation per Unix 300-second slot. Persist exact response bytes, SHA-256, request and receipt UTC Unix times, exchange timestamp, best bid, best ask, absolute spread (USDT/BTC); relative spread in bp = 10000*(ask-bid)/mid. Accepted quotes require the correct market, finite positive bid <= ask, exchange time within 60 seconds before receipt, request and receipt in the same slot. Use only first committed observation per slot. Never backfill. Missing or rejected observations are gaps. Verify raw hashes and rederive quote fields before evaluation; archive a consistent SQLite backup read-only and record its SHA-256, software commit, expected/valid slots and longest gap in the final result commit. SQLite insert-only protection and local hashes detect accidental edits; they do not provide independent third-party timestamping.

## Frozen decision and accounting rules

* Use UTC days with all 288 valid slots. Daily high, low and close are the maximum, minimum and final sampled midpoint. These are sampled ranges, not exchange OHLC. For entry require 21 consecutive complete prior days. Compute sample standard deviation of the 20 daily log close returns, annualized with sqrt(365).
* Start flat with 1000 USDT. At each valid slot while flat, trigger BUY only on an upward crossing of yesterday's high: immediately preceding slot mid <= high and current mid > high. At most one attempted entry per UTC day. No leverage, short, pyramiding or rebalance. If volatility is zero or unavailable, HOLD. Target spend including fees = current cash * min(0.25, 0.08 / annualized volatility).
* Model entry at the immediately following valid scheduled slot (exactly +1 slot, exchange timestamp strictly after signal receipt); otherwise cancel entry. Pay ask times (1 + slippage), fee on the buy quote amount; BTC = spend / [effective ask * (1 + fee)]. Permit fractional model units. Require sufficient displayed top ask size; otherwise skip. Baseline fee assumption 0.10% per side and 5 bp slippage per side; stress 0.20% and 25 bp. Observed spread is already in bid/ask.
* Exit signal at first valid mid <= 97% of entry mid, or at the first valid sample at least 24h after entry, whichever comes first. Model full exit at first later valid bid with exchange time after decision receipt and sufficient displayed size; proceeds = units * bid * (1 - slippage) * (1 - fee). No same-slot re-entry. Otherwise HOLD. At end value any remaining position at last valid liquidation bid; exclude this open position from completed trades and PF. No new entries in final 24h.
* At every valid sample mark equity at model liquidation proceeds plus cash. Track peak from 1000 and maximum peak-to-trough percentage. A baseline drawdown >10% immediately disqualifies the strategy; do not assume a stop caps gap losses. Report cash and same-window buy-and-hold (first eligible ask to final bid, same costs), number of completed round trips, profit factor (sum positive net trade PnL / absolute sum negative), net return and sampled drawdown for both cost scenarios.

## Decision, once

Data integrity gate: verify all hashes used, >=99% valid slots over the whole window, final slot valid and no gap >15 minutes while exposed. Any integrity failure yields INCONCLUSIVE, no admission. On valid data, reject for net return <=0%, max drawdown >10%, PF <=1.2, or stress return <=0%. Require at least 30 closed round trips; a smaller sample cannot pass. For convincing evidence additionally require a positive one-sided 95% lower bound on mean daily net return: circular moving-block bootstrap, 7-day blocks, 10000 draws, Python random.Random(3003), resample consecutive daily liquidation-equity returns after warm-up to original length and use sorted mean at index 499. Use last valid daily mark, carrying previous equity only for wholly missing days; disclose counts. Unsupported statistical evidence yields INCONCLUSIVE. No-loss PF is infinite only with positive realized gains. Undefined metrics cannot pass. Record final verdict and evidence in Git before any separate admission decision. Revisions require a new ID and a new future window.
